# Harley Bradford

Director of Marketing & Sales | Systems & Growth  
Great Yarmouth, England, United Kingdom

---

## About Me

I work at the intersection of **systems, strategy, and growth**, helping early-stage businesses, startups, and non-profits turn complexity into clarity.

My core belief is simple: **good organisations are built on strong operating systems**. Marketing, sales, and strategy only scale when the underlying processes, workflows, and structures are sound.

I currently serve as **Director of Marketing & Sales at Ashdown Digital Solutions**, where I set and execute commercial strategy, oversee marketing performance, and coordinate the full sales lifecycle from lead generation through to onboarding and relationship management. Alongside this, I advise organisations on operational design, process mapping, and user-centred systems that support sustainable growth.

I am motivated by work that creates order where there is ambiguity, particularly in environments where resources are constrained but ambition is high.

---

## What I Do

### Systems & Operations
I design and improve operating systems that align day-to-day execution with long-term intent. This includes mapping processes, building workflows, and helping teams move from informal coordination to repeatable, dependable delivery.

### Marketing & Sales
I lead marketing and sales as connected systems, not isolated functions. My focus is on clarity of message, consistency of execution, and ensuring commercial activity supports long-term organisational goals rather than short-term metrics.
